# 🇲🇦 YASSINZN - Moroccan Mini-Games (2-3-4 Players)

**YASSINZN** hiya lo3ba maghribiya m2addada b-HTML5, CSS3, w JavaScript. Kat-smeḥ l-2, 3, aw 4 d l-lo3aba y-l3bo f nafs l-wa9t 3la nafs l-jihaz (Mobile wla PC) b-anwa3 mghtalfa dyal l-mini-games b-jaw maghribi wa3er!

---

## 🌟 Mmmayizat l-Lo3ba (Features)

- **🕹️ Multi-Touch Controls**: Boutonat d l-touch kbar w wadhin f l-ecran d t-tele kiy-smeho l 4 d l-lo3aba y-l3bo f nafs l-wa9t بلا ما y-bloqa l-input.
- **⌨️ Keyboard Support**: التحكم b-l-kiewboard l kiy-l3bo f PC (WASD, Arrows, IJKL, Numpad).
- **🤖 Solo Mode (VS AI)**: L-la3ib dhki (AI) kiy-l3b m3ak ila konti bouhdek.
- **🛡️ Team Mode (2v2)**: L-lo3b b-fari9 ضد fari9 f-lo3ab bhal Koura w Dabbaba.
- **🏆 Tournament Mode (البطولة)**: Siba9 d l-niqat bin l-lo3aba b-classement w podium f l-lekher.
- **📱 Offline / PWA Ready**: Kat-khdem bila internet mni kat-tzad l l-shasha r-ra2isiya.

---

## 🎮 L-Lo3ab li f- l-Mashrou3 (Mini-Games List)

1. **⚽ Koura (Football)**: Match d koura b-potowat wadhin w physics d l-kora m9addad.
2. **🛡️ Dabbaba (Tank)**: Dabbaba kat-dor 360° - wrrek 3la l-bouton bash t-tiri w t-zzed l-9ddam!
3. **🕊️ L-7mam (Pigeon)**: Flappy style b-bouton waḥda d t-n9az.
4. **🚖 L-Taxi (Taxi Race)**: Siba9 f l-zn9a m3a t-fadi l-tonobilat b-زرين (Limin / Liser).
5. **🚀 S-Sarokh (Rocket)**: Tle3 f s-sma w t-fada l-ahjar m3a ziada d s-sor3a.
6. **🐑 L-Khrof (Sheep Jump)**: N9ez 3la l-7wajez b-bouton waḥda f-ajwa2 l-3id.
7. **🍲 L-Tajine (Tajine Memory)**: Lo3ba d t-takfkir! 3andek 5s bash t-3qel 3la 4 d l-mokawinat w t-3ammar l-tajine dyalek s-sḥiḥ.
8. **🛵 Scooter Racing**: Siba9 d 3 d d-dawrat (3 Laps) f-7alaqa d s-siba9.
9. **🚩 L-3alam (Flag Capture)**: Khdef l-3alam dyal l-khsem m3a l-imkaniya d t-tīr 3la l-3ddow.
10. **♟️ Chatranj (Chess)**: Lo3ba d l-3aql b-Crono w wa9t moḥaddad (3-4 min) l-kol la3ib.
11. **🔴 L-Dama (Checkers)**: Dama b-9awa3idha s-saḥiḥa (t-n9az l-ijbari w l-malika).
12. **🔢 Sudoku**: Lo3ba d t-tafkir w l-2ar9am b-wa9t moḥaddad.
